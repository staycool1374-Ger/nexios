## SETTLE TERMINATION / SAFETY REVIEW (task-pointed)
- Termination bound: `settle_deadline = ticks()+500` with `while (ticks() <
  deadline)`; `Timer::ticks()` is monotonic u64 ⇒ hard bound, no wrap, cannot
  spin forever on a permanently non-empty inbox.
- `IPC::recv` drain of init's own inbox (kernel.cpp:574-576): SAFE. At this
  boot point the daemon-ready handshake (:400-469) has already drained every
  MSG_DAEMON_READY/FAILED; init's only other inbox consumer is the post-suite
  reaper loop (:692-729) which runs AFTER the suite, not before the settle.
  `IPC::recv` also `wake_sender`s any blocked sender, so draining cannot wedge
  a producer. Dropping these tail messages loses nothing a later path needs.
- `all_tasks()` iteration correctness: `first_ptr`/`next_ptr` walk an
  intrusive per-priority list; TCB blocks are pin-stable (snapshot pin loop
  precedes) and magic-gated, so traversal cannot dereference a recycled block.
- `arch::hlt()` at init prio 10 with daemons prio 20: CORRECT. priority_map
  treats the numerically-highest set level as highest priority, so the prio-20
  daemons preempt init on the next tick and consume their grants — the exact
  established pattern already used at kernel.cpp:468. IF=1 in this context
  (comment :543) so hlt wakes on the timer IRQ.
- Atomicity argument (detector): SOUND on the BSP — scan and capture are in
  the same IF=0 window of one `snapshot_create()` call.
- No NEW defect from the rewrite: no dynamic allocation, no lock taken by the
  settle (reads atomic `count`), no assert weakened, no preprocessor
  asymmetry (create/restore/accessor all CONFIG_DEBUG-consistent).
