## FINDINGS
- [S2] docs/specs/microkernel-transition.md:620 — new false claim. The text says `reap_orphans` reaps self-exited tasks "only once the parent is dead or gone".
  WHY: `task/scheduler_lifecycle.cpp:Scheduler::reap_orphans` sets `can_reap` in three cases:
  - `parent_id == 0`
  - the parent is a RUNNING PID 1
  - the parent is TERMINATED or absent from the id table

  Most daemons and PDs are children of PID 1, so the stated restriction is wrong on the common path.
