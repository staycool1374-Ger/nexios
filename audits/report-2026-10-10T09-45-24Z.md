## ASSESSMENT OF THE SETTLE (keep vs drop)
Recommendation: KEEP as a structural guarantee, do not drop. The change is
bounded (≤500 ticks), DEBUG-instrumented, allocation-free, lock-free, and makes
the baseline IPC-quiescent by construction rather than by scheduling luck —
dropping it reverts to an unguaranteed baseline for a class of correctness
landmine (non-idempotent receiver replay). However it is UNPROVEN (no
reproduction) and its test is vacuous, so it must not be presented as
demonstrated. Required follow-up: a deterministic test that exercises a
non-empty baseline and asserts non-replay after `snapshot_restore` (or an
explicit acknowledgement in the spec that the settle is a preventive guarantee,
not a fix for an observed defect).
