## FINDINGS
- [S2] docs/specs/microkernel-transition.md:§9.1 and INV-MK-6 row: the DEATH-1 evidence is incomplete.
  WHY: The spec misses several facts:
  - `on_task_death` runs in `TaskControlBlock::complete_teardown` (reached from `cleanup()` and `destroy()`).
  - `syscall_handlers_process.cpp:sys_waitpid` calls `child->cleanup()` synchronously.
  - Self-exit (`sys_exit` → `switch_away_from_terminating`) never calls `release_zombie`. Such tasks are therefore reached by `reap_orphans` → `note_termination_and_free` (every 100 ticks from `on_tick`).
  - `drain_zombie_list` is driven by the priority-0 PID 1 reaper (`kernel.cpp`).

  The "runs only from" list and the blanket "unbounded" conclusion therefore misstate the death path that the M1/F6 requirement is built on.
- [S2] docs/specs/microkernel-transition.md:§7.2, INV-MK-1, §8.2(5): `CONFIG_STATIC_POOLS_ONLY` is claimed to forbid PMM allocation after init.
  WHY: `memory/pmm.cpp` asserts only in `alloc_page`, `alloc_contiguous` and `alloc_page_colored`. `alloc_user_page`, `alloc_user_contiguous` and `alloc_page_table` are ungated, and every listed violation site uses them:
  - `sys_frame_create`: `alloc_user_contiguous`
  - `BufferPool::alloc_page`: `alloc_user_page`
  - `elf_loader`: `alloc_user_*`
  - `vmm`: `alloc_user_page` / `alloc_page_table`

  The stated enforcement point cannot detect the stated violations.
- [S2] docs/specs/microkernel-transition.md:§11 "Level-line mask on delivery" row and §8.3(1): the per-arch parity claim is wrong.
  WHY: `irq_delivery.cpp:mask_on_delivery` runs for WAIT_ACK only, on both x86_64 and aarch64. aarch64 WAIT mode is not masked either. riscv64 deliberately never masks: the PLIC comment says "do NOT mask on delivery", so "PLIC claim/complete" is not a mask equivalent. On x86, every PIC-kind arm programs a level IOAPIC entry.
- [S2] docs/specs/microkernel-transition.md:§8.3(1): the claim "`IRQ_WAIT` registration overwrites a live waiter" is false.
  WHY: `sys_irq_wait` refuses `r->recipient != t` under the slot lock, so no second distinct task can register. The single-waiter rule is hardening, not a defect fix. F8 and M8 must not cite it as a convicted defect.
- [S2] docs/specs/microkernel-transition.md:T-1 rows 58 and 75, and §2 IPC row: the waits are labelled "bounded".
  WHY: `sys_irq_wait` arg1, `sys_recv_fast` arg3 and `sys_receive` arg3 all use 0 = block forever. In a SIL 3 spec, calling an optionally unbounded wait "bounded" misstates a safety property that INV-MK-3 depends on.
- [S2] docs/specs/microkernel-transition.md:header and §8, §11, §13, §14: v0.6.1 milestone coverage is incomplete.
  WHY: Five open milestone-#18 issues are not covered: #398 (IPC fastpath, which is M3), #515 (cap-broker policy serving), #528 (aarch64 ioport/IOMMU dispatch test failures, a parity gap), #544 (riscv64 KEEP-D debug-syscall flake) and #89 (v0.6.1 test-coverage tracker). The #512 plan itself names #515, #528, #544 and #89.
