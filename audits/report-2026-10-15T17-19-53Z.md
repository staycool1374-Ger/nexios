  huge/block entries) mutate nothing and skip the flush. The removed

  legacy, `user=false`), i.e. fail-open kernel NULL-deref alias. The new
  clear-leaf encoding is fail-closed (#PF). Safety rationale in `vmm.hpp`
  docs is accurate.

  `mmio.cpp:239,280,304`): all call sites are teardown/rollback/revoke

  returns 0 both for not-present and for a phys-0 leaf, so no caller could
