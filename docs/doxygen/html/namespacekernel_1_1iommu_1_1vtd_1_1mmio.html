Detailed Description

VT-d MMIO register offsets relative to the remapping-unit base (spec §10.4, matching Linux intel-iommu.h and QEMU). VER_REG at 0x0, CAP at 0x8, ECAP at 0x10, GCMD at 0x18, GSTS at 0x1c, RTADDR at 0x20, CCMD at 0x28, FSTS at 0x34, FECTL at 0x38, IQH at 0x80, IQT at 0x88, IQA at 0x90.
