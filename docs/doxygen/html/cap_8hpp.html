int kernel::cap::retype (CNode *cspace, uint64_t untyped_handle, CapType target_type, size_t size, uint32_t rights) noexcept
 Retypes the Untyped at untyped_handle in cspace into a new capability of target_type, installed into cspace. Supports Frame targets with a sub-range carve (issue #1): size may be smaller than the Untyped's region (PAGE-aligned, prefix-only); the remainder is installed as a child Untyped in the same cspace and is itself retypable (exhaustion model). size == ut->size is the exact-size degenerate case (no child). Non-destructive on validation/capacity failure; fail-closed after the guard is claimed.
